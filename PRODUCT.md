# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Northeastern students browsing courses, checking degree requirements, and planning semesters around completed courses, co-op, and graduation goals. Source: README.md.

## Product Purpose

Bring course descriptions, prerequisites, degree requirements, and published sample plans into one workspace. Students can browse courses, request plain-English explanations, and build a semester timeline. Source: README.md.

## Brand Personality

Warm, quiet, and clear. The workspace uses restrained surfaces and thin lines, with red reserved for small signals. Source: docs/DESIGN.md.

## Anti-references

Avoid feature-card grids, glass effects, glows, decorative workspace elements, gradients, and AI-powered badges. Do not imply Northeastern endorsement. Source: docs/DESIGN.md.

## Design Principles

- Keep course browsing and planning useful without AI or an API key.
- Preserve catalog choices, alternatives, and course bundles.
- Keep program controls from hiding course browsing.
- Use concise explanations and familiar controls.
- Ground requirements and planning context in published source data.

Sources: README.md and docs/DESIGN.md.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Provide visible focus rings, touch targets of at least 44px, associated labels, and one status region per window. Respect reduced motion. On narrow screens, use a single page-scrolling column. Source: docs/DESIGN.md.
