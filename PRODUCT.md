# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Northeastern students searching the course catalog, reading prerequisite rules, and deciding which courses to discuss with an academic advisor. They need plain-English explanations and published sample-plan context when available.

## Product Purpose

NU Insights combines catalog descriptions, prerequisite trees, corequisites, NUpath attributes, and sample-plan placement with optional AI breakdowns. The main workflow is search, select a course, inspect its details, and request a breakdown. Browsing remains available without an AI key.

## Brand Personality

Warm, quiet, practical. Use clear student-focused language, with the tone of an upperclassman explaining a course. Preserve the existing visual direction documented in DESIGN.md.

## Anti-references

Avoid feature-card grids, glass effects, glows, gradients, numbered marketing sections, decorative workspace elements, and AI-powered badges. Do not imply official Northeastern endorsement.

## Design Principles

- Ground course explanations in the supplied catalog data.
- Preserve prerequisite AND/OR meaning and distinguish mandatory courses from alternatives.
- Label historical sample plans with their catalog year. Never present them as current requirements, live course offerings, or an eligibility check.
- Explain missing dataset coverage separately from missing placement for one course.
- Keep the course-search workflow predictable and the interface focused on reading.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Retain visible focus rings, associated input labels, semantic headings, minimum 44px touch targets, responsive single-column layouts, and reduced-motion support. Use text to convey meaning rather than relying on color alone.

This context records the existing README and DESIGN.md direction and the user's explicit choice to show clearly labeled historical 2025–26 plans.
